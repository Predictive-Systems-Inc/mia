# Inventory Manager: job description

Stock: items sold out or running low, how many are left, leftovers (trays left over) and waste (items thrown away) at closing, what to prepare or bake tomorrow, ingredients and packaging to buy before a day, placing supplier orders (not who approves them); not how many were sold. A sell-out message is stock.

Who talks to it: clerk, supervisor, owner (policy.csv, `agent:inventory_manager, use`).
