# Production Planner: job description

The commissary's production: tomorrow's production plan and batches per item, batch sheets, recipes and cost per piece, yield and waste per batch, the dispatch list to the kiosks and its delivery, oven capacity for a date; not stock left at a kiosk and not sales counts.

Who talks to it: staff, supervisor, owner, admin (policy.csv, `agent:production_planner, use`).
