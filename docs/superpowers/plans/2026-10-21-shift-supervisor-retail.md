# Shift Supervisor Retail Copy Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

- **Goal:** Ferino's clerks and head staff get the shift supervisor at the kiosk: the week's roster from the owner's pattern, check-in and check-out at the counter, a sick or late clerk and the cover from the other kiosk, a swap, a corrected time, a broken stove or a missed delivery, the weekly roster on Sunday and a kiosk closed for a holiday; the cleaning company keeps the same agent unchanged.
- **Architecture:** not a new agent (D39, rule 15): the same package `node/mia/agents/shift_supervisor/` with a template switch, `rules.is_retail()` (`orgconfig.load().template == "retail"`) for logic and `replies.word_key(key)` for words. One pure module `roster.py` (pattern, ranges, days, job names, swap checks). Kiosk shifts are core Visits (the branch's one Location, one Job per clerk and pattern, one visit per clerk). Data standard 1.7 adds the incident kinds; the retail template adds `retail_closures`. Tool and intent ids stay as they are, even where they say visit or site.
- **Tech Stack:** as the bookkeeper. **Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-shift-supervisor-retail-design.md (binding; D39 answers Q101 to Q103).
