## Global Constraints
The sales supervisor, HR officer and branch manager plans' lists apply, plus these exact values:
- `orgconfig.BookkeeperSettings` (read through `orgconfig.load().bookkeeper`): `threshold: int = 10_000` (100 pesos; one threshold for the cash count, settlements and payouts; flagged only when `abs(gap) > threshold`), `expense_limit: int = 500_000` (5,000 pesos; approval only when `amount > expense_limit`), `fees: dict[str, int] = {"gcash": 2, "maya": 2}`, `commissions: dict[str, int] = {"grab": 25, "foodpanda": 28}` (integer percents, placeholders the owner corrects), `tax_regime: Literal["vat", "percentage"] = "vat"`, `petty_floor: int = 50_000` (500 pesos), `undeposited_limit: int = 2_000_000` (20,000 pesos; the reminder after two business dates in a row above it). The spec gives no figures for the last two: these are assumptions in Q90.
- `tax.py` (`TABLES_AS_OF = "2026-01"`, verify with the accountant as Q61): `RATES = (Rate("vat", dt.date(2006, 2, 1), 12), Rate("percentage", dt.date(2020, 7, 1), 1), Rate("percentage", dt.date(2023, 7, 1), 3))`; VAT on VAT-inclusive gross: `output = half_up(gross * 12 / 112)`, `taxable = gross - output`, `due = output - input` (negative is an excess carried over, the reply says so); percentage tax `due = half_up(gross * 3 / 100)`, input ignored; the deadline is the quarter's end plus 25 days (BIR 2550Q and 2551Q). Q88 assumed: every sale is VAT-able. Q89 assumed: a receipt without a typed VAT amount carries no input VAT.
- Fixed lists in `words.py`: TENDERS (cash, gcash, maya, grab, foodpanda; `mia.core.models.TENDERS`, the code list of the standard), SETTLERS (gcash, maya), PLATFORMS (grab, foodpanda), CATEGORIES (utilities, rent, supplies, transport, repairs, other), BANKS (bdo, bpi, metrobank, landbank, unionbank, security_bank, chinabank, pnb, rcbc, petty), PERIODS (today, yesterday, this_week, last_week, this_month, last_month, this_quarter, last_quarter; weeks run Monday to Sunday). A petty refill is a deposit with bank `petty` (cash leaves the drawer into the petty box): petty balance = refills less spends with source petty; undeposited cash = counted cash less every deposit less spends with source cash, per business date, the latest count of a date winning.
- Money is integer minor units (PHP) with a currency; percents round half up; the price parser (`parsing.find_prices`) converts once. Rule 7: every amount is typed by a person and parsed by code; Jev picks the action, the amount id (`p0`, `p1`), the provider, the category, the period and the bank among code's candidates, never a number. No person candidates in v1 (they served utang, Q86).
- Profit (cash basis, Q90 assumed): sales (every tender) less fees and commissions less expenses less supplier spend (received `supplier_orders` totals by business date) less payroll (gross of `payslips` whose `cutoff_end` falls in the month, summed per branch, never a row per person). Cost of goods (units sold times `retail_items.cost`, None while any sold item has no cost, Q79) and waste at cost are shown as lines and not subtracted, since supplier spend already paid for them.
- Rule 3: `record_large_expense` is `risk: money, approval: always`, approvers `[owner, admin]` (app_only, step-up); `record_expense` refuses `amount > expense_limit` in its precheck (`LookupError("over_limit")`), so no path writes a large expense without the card. The owner's own large expense is decided by the owner with a fresh step-up (D37, core rule); a supervisor never decides their own.
- Payslip totals: only `monthly_profit` reads them, it checks `read_all` on `data:payslips` (owner and admin only; `read_own` gates by role, so it is never used here) and is `sensitive: true` (replies and notifications keep the totals off the chain). A supervisor sees their branch only and never a payroll figure.
- Ten languages (`i18n.SUPPORTED`), Filipino (fil) first, never "Tagalog"; no em-dashes; ids from `MANIFEST.id` (rule 15); aware times (`TZDateTime`), dates from the branch's local clock (`retail_hours.business_date_at`); `0000_initial` edited in place; tests pin clocks through `bk_pin`, never the wall clock.
- **Stop rule (data standard):** Task 1 renames tables and adds one column. If it needs anything else in core (a field change, a new FK, a dropped redaction, a right added or dropped for a human role), stop and report before writing it.
