# Production Planner v1 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

- **Goal:** Ferino's commissary head gets tomorrow's production plan at 14:00 (per item the batches, the split per kiosk from each kiosk's forecast and confirmed customer orders), batch sheets with cost per batch and per piece, yield and waste per batch, the dispatch list per kiosk confirmed by the driver and matched to the kiosk's receipt, and the ovens' free capacity for a date.
- **Architecture:** Task 1 first promotes `customer_service_orders` to core `customer_orders` (data standard 1.7, ADR 019, D39). Then `uv run mia agent scaffold production_planner --label-en "Production Planner" --label-fi "Tuotannonsuunnittelija" --role staff --role supervisor --role owner --role admin` creates `node/mia/agents/production_planner/` (no stub exists) in the bookkeeper's shape: four agent-owned tables, pure `forecast.py` and `capacity.py`, `words.py` (number, batch and recipe readers), actions, decider, nine tools (none through approvals), replies, due work. `staff` is the driver (Q94); a clerk inherits staff, so a deny line keeps clerks off the agent (spec section 3).
- **Tech Stack:** as the bookkeeper. **Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-production-planner-design.md (binding; D39 answers Q91, Q92 and Q96). Line anchors are as of adabeaa; the bookkeeper and the retail shift supervisor are being built, so re-find an anchor by its symbol when it moved.

## Global Constraints
The bookkeeper plan's list applies, plus these exact values:
- `orgconfig.ProductionPlannerSettings` (read through `orgconfig.load().production_planner`): `commissary: str = ""` (empty: the agent does nothing), `serves: list[str] = []`, `ovens: int = 2`, `hours: ProductionHours(start: dt.time = dt.time(3, 0), end: dt.time = dt.time(9, 0))`, `plan_at: dt.time = dt.time(14, 0)`, `dispatch_at: dt.time = dt.time(8, 30)`. Ferino's: `commissary: Commissary`, `serves: [Mall kiosk, Street stall]`. Capacity at Ferino's: 2 ovens times 360 minutes = 720 oven minutes a day.
- Forecast (`forecast.py`, rule 7): `FORECAST_DAYS = 28`, `MIN_FORECAST_DAYS = 2`. Per kiosk and item: units on the dates in `[day - 28, day)` with the production date's weekday on which the kiosk has any live count (`superseded_by` is None, tender ignored), bundles expanded through `retail_items.components`, the mean as an exact `Fraction` rounded half up; fewer than 2 such dates is None ("not enough data", the line asks the head for a number, its demand is the orders alone). Demand = forecast + confirmed order pieces (status `confirmed`, pickup on that local date at that kiosk, bundles expanded) - expected leftover (the last `leftover` stock event of the item at the kiosk when the recipe `keeps_overnight`, else 0; Q93 assumed: none keep), never below 0.
- Batches per item = ceil(commissary total demand / `batch_yield`), plus one when `inventory_manager.tools.plan_lines` says `more` for the item at any served kiosk; pieces = batches times yield; the surplus goes to the kiosk with the larger forecast (None counts as 0; a tie goes to the first in `serves`). A dispatch quantity is `forecast.dispatch_split(good, planned)`: recorded batches count their `good`, unrecorded ones their planned yield; a shortfall comes off the kiosk with the larger planned pieces first, extra pieces go to it. Lines of 0 are left out.
- Capacity (`capacity.py`): `minutes_free = max(0, ovens * window minutes - sum(batches * oven_minutes))`; free batches of an item = `minutes_free // oven_minutes`, free pieces = that times `batch_yield` (ponytail: one pool of oven minutes, no per-oven packing; the upgrade is a schedule per oven). Seeded recipes: Classic and Puto bumbong 48 pieces, the others 36, every one 45 oven minutes (made up, the owner corrects them).
- Costs: a line costs `half_up(quantity_milli * pack_price / 1000)`; cost per batch is the sum of the lines for one batch, cost per piece `half_up(cost_per_batch / batch_yield)`; an ingredient without `pack_price` shows no cost and makes both totals None (Q55's rule: never a partial cost shown as whole). Money is integer minor units (PHP) with a currency; no tool is money, external or delete, so no approval and D37 does not apply; the recipe card is a confirmation (a stored decision), not an approval.
- Prechecks: `change_plan` refuses `LookupError("capacity")` when the new batches need more than `minutes_free` (the reply names the free minutes) and `LookupError("past")` for a production date before the commissary's production day; `record_batch` refuses `LookupError("not_planned")` for an item or batch number outside the day's plan and `LookupError("batch_total")` when `good + waste > 2 * batch_yield`; a repeated batch number updates its row (a correction).
- The production day (`tools.production_day(commissary, settings, now)`): the commissary's local date while local time < `hours.end`, else the next date. The dispatch confirmation rule: one dispatch per kiosk and production date, BranchScoped on the kiosk with `from_branch_id` the commissary; only the driver (staff at the commissary with skill `driver`) or the head (a supervisor at the commissary) taps "Delivered" per kiosk (else `LookupError("not_driver")`); at the kiosk's opening plus 60 minutes `match_tick` compares each line with the kiosk's `received` stock events with that `item_id` and business date (Q95: no dispatch id): every line equal is `matched` and quiet; any line different, or no receipt, is `short`, told to the head and `alerts.leads`.
- Rights (D39, Q96): the agent role reads `sales_counts`, `stock_events` and `customer_orders` of the served kiosks with `read_all`, always as the agent alone (`tools.agent_actor(branch_id)`, no `on_behalf_of`), after the kiosk is checked against `serves`; no human role gains a cross-branch right. The head reads and writes plan rows at its own branch (`read`, `write`); a kiosk supervisor's `plan` and `dispatch_list` are filtered to their kiosk (rows by `branch_id`, plan lines by kiosk); the driver reads dispatches `from_branch_id` their branch (`read_own` gates by role only, said where used); owner and admin `read_all` and `write_all`, and they alone `set_recipe`. The planner never reads `customer_service_customers`, `delivery_note` or any order text: only `lines`, `status`, `pickup_at`, `branch_id`.
- Rule 7 everywhere: Jev picks the action, the item, the kiosk, the direction (add, remove, set), the number id (`n0`, `n1`), the unit (batches, pieces) and the date id among code's candidates, never a number. Ten languages (`i18n.SUPPORTED`), Filipino (fil) first, never "Tagalog"; no em-dashes; ids from `MANIFEST.id` (rule 15); aware times (`TZDateTime`); `0000_initial` edited in place; tests pin clocks through `pp_pin`, never the wall clock.
- **Stop rule (data standard):** Task 1 renames one table and its indexes. If it needs anything else in core (a field change, a column on `branch` or `stock_events`, a dropped redaction, a right added or dropped for a human role), stop and report before writing it.
