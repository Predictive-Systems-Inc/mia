# Sales Receipts in the Chat Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

- **Goal:** on a kiosk set up for receipts (`sales.receipts_from`), the chat is the till (Q119): a clerk's check-in opens her sales session, she rings up receipts by text ("resibo 3 ube cash 135"), a void raises head staff's card and a refund the owner's or admin's, head staff's closing count closes the drawer against its expected cash, and the reads and the owner's report come from receipts. A counts kiosk keeps today's behaviour. The owner's words: "Can we now create full end to end operation of sales operations. From reporting of sales to cash count", and on sources "We can support both, setup per kiosk".
- **Architecture:** no new agent (rule 15). Three hook points call `mia/core/sales.py` (data standard 1.9, ADR 022, D43): the shift supervisor's `check_in` tool opens the session, the bookkeeper's `record_cash_count` tool closes it, and the sales supervisor gains four tools (`record_receipt`, `void_receipt`, `refund_receipt`, `receipts`). The two money tools go through the approvals guard (rule 3) with a binding-level request hook so the approval carries the core types `sales.void` and `sales.refund` that `core.sales._approved` checks. The kiosk's source (`counts` or `receipts`) is read by code from the settings, never by Jev: Jev picks the action, code turns a receipt into a count on a counts kiosk and a count into a receipt on a receipts kiosk.
- **Tech Stack:** as the bookkeeper plan (Python 3.12, FastAPI, SQLModel, Pydantic, Jev through the shared decider, fake Jev in tests). **Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-sales-operations-design.md (sections 4 to 9 and 12, binding), docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-sales-supervisor-design.md (the agent), D43 and D42 in docs/decisions.md, Q119 in docs/questions.md.

## Global Constraints
The bookkeeper, sales supervisor and retail shift supervisor plans' lists apply, plus these exact values:
- **Rule 7, every number by code.** Prices are the dated price (`retail_items.price_on`, the row from `price_row_on`) at the business date of the receipt; totals, the refund amount, expected cash and differences are computed in `core.sales` or the tools. Jev picks the action, the items (`item_<i>` over the branch menu, as counts do) and `receipts_which`; never a number, a tender or a receipt number. Typed totals are whole pesos converted once (`decider._minor`, times 100).
- **Rule 8, the message is data.** The message goes to Jev as state only; a void or refund reason is stored as data (`void_reason`, evidence `reason`, first 200 characters) and never read as an instruction.
- **Rule 11.** No new await. `_jev_turn` already commits before the Jev request; every write here runs in `_act` (worker thread) or an approval handler (the decide transaction), and `call_tool` commits after the tool. The receipt number is computed after `events.write_lock(session)` in the same transaction as the write.
- **Writes through core.sales only.** `sales_sessions`, `sales_receipts`, `sales_receipt_lines` and `sales_payments` are written only by `sales.open_session`, `close_session`, `record_receipt` and `void_receipt` (and the new `open_for_shift`, which calls `open_session`). A `SalesError` becomes `LookupError(<its code>)` in the tools so `call_tool` answers `invalid`.
- **Rule 3 for the two money tools.** `sales_supervisor.void_receipt` and `sales_supervisor.refund_receipt` are `risk: money, approval: always`; the guard asks first (app only, step-up, the requester never decides their own; D37 lets an owner decide their own money request with a fresh PIN, the core rule, unchanged). The approval type is `sales.void` (approvers `[supervisor, admin, owner]`) or `sales.refund` (`[admin, owner]`), subject `("sales_receipts", <receipt id>)`, `evidence["amount"]` the receipt's total (void) or the computed refund amount (refund). An approved request runs its handler once; `core.sales` refuses a second use.
